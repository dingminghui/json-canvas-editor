# Narrative modes

Choose exactly one mode:

- `pyramid`: Lead with the decision or conclusion, then support it with reasons, evidence, and action. Use for approvals and executive decisions.
- `narrative`: Move through situation, tension, turning point, resolution, and future. Use for vision, change, and storytelling.
- `instructional`: Move through objective, concept, steps, example, and verification. Use for training and tutorials.
- `showcase`: Move through positioning, capabilities, scenarios, proof, and call to action. Use for products and portfolios.
- `briefing`: Move through summary, status, issues, decisions, and next steps. Use for project and information updates.

Select from audience, objective, delivery context, and source texture. Do not ask the user to choose an internal mode when the intent already makes the choice clear.
